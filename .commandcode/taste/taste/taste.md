# Taste
- Wants maximal, immersive UI: abundant animations, rich styling/shine effects, "feature-bombed" pages — plain or sparse designs are rejected as "too less". Explicitly wants a look "no one has ever created" — unprecedented/original designs, not conventional or off-the-shelf UI. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers a single strong immersive landing page whose visual language then spreads across the whole site. Confidence: 0.7
- Likes gamified/themed experiences (game and movie themes) that users emotionally relate to, not exact copies but an original take on them. Confidence: 0.6
- Prefers multi-page sites with multiple templates and layouts over a single-page site; expects all pages to be brought up to the same redesigned quality, not just one. Confidence: 0.6
- Wants real, verifiable content instead of placeholder or "bad" data — e.g. actual public repos listed with proper descriptions, data seeded from real proof, certificates/timeline pulled from real source material. Confidence: 0.75
- Wants unwanted/stale content and database records cleaned out rather than left in place. Confidence: 0.6
- Prefers creating a new branch before attempting experimental or risky rework. Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers free / free-tier tooling and cost avoidance — e.g. free Groq Whisper for transcription and rotating multiple Gemini API keys to stay within free limits rather than paying. Confidence: 0.55
- Cares about output/asset quality — wants the best-quality version of visual assets (e.g. full resolution, minimal compression, "best quality") and likes to compare several candidate options before picking the final one. Confidence: 0.55
